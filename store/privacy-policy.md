## Chrome extension

The Grassy Knees extension for Chrome shows this weekend's kids' events from grassyknees.com. It collects no personal data and has no analytics or tracking.

- **What it stores, on your computer only:** the metro area you picked and your filter choices (Free only, child's age) are kept in Chrome's extension storage. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome syncs them. A copy of your metro's weekend list is kept for about 30 minutes so the extension opens quickly. Removing the extension deletes all of this.
- **What it sends:** requests for public listings to grassyknees.com, with no cookies or account information. The Detect button loads the grassyknees.com homepage, and our server names the metro nearest the visitor from Cloudflare's IP-based estimate. That location is not stored. As with any website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) handles the request, including your IP address, as described above.
- **What it doesn't do:** it doesn't read or change the pages you visit, it doesn't see your browsing history, it doesn't use Chrome's location permission, and it shares nothing with anyone else. Links you open carry `utm_source=chrome-extension` so our own page counts can show the visit came from the extension.
